# Welcome to my blog

Hey there! This is the first post on my new blog. I'm Hakim, a Computer Science
student and full-stack developer based in Kuala Lumpur. I decided to start
writing to solidify what I learn and share it with others.

## What I'll write about

Expect posts on:

- **Full-stack development** — React, Next.js, SvelteKit, Node.js
- **Scalable architecture** — databases, APIs, and deployment
- **Things I'm learning** — new tools, interesting bugs, and trade-offs

## Why a blog

Writing forces me to understand things properly. If I can't explain a concept,
I probably don't know it well enough. Plus, it's nice to look back and see how
much I've grown.
